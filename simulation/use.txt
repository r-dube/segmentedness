The code included here generates the tables and figures in section 6 (Estimator Evaluation) of the https://www.researchgate.net/publication/400704460_How_segmented_is_my_network
The purpose of the code is to demonstrate that the estimator proposed in the paper produces the theoretically predicted output.
ER.ipynb corresponds to section 6.1 (Estimation over Random Networks)
SBM-equal-sized-blocks.ipynb corresponds to section 6.2 (Estimation over Networks with Communities)
CSW-analysis.ipynb corresponds to section 6.3 (Estimation over Enterprise Network Data)
value-weighted.ipynb corresponds to the appendix of https://www.researchgate.net/publication/410040876_Value-Weighted_Segmentedness_Measuring_Whether_a_Network_Isolates_What_Matters
